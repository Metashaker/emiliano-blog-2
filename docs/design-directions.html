Thirteen Ways of Looking at a Blog

The same post rendered in thirteen visual directions — spanning pure editorial (every.to), technical precision (ampcode), and mythic dark atmosphere (Hermes), blended toward one brief: Greek, in space, in a cyberpunk world. Pick the feel; we build it.

These use the real target typefaces (Fraunces, Cormorant, Cinzel, Newsreader, Orbitron, Chakra Petch, Sora, IBM Plex Mono, Marcellus, Audiowide), embedded directly. Every tile shows the identical article so you compare style, not content.

every.to

Editorial rigor — literary serif, generous measure, near-colorless restraint.

ampcode

Technical precision — mono labels, high contrast, a single hot accent.

Hermes

Mythic atmosphere — dark cosmic ground, gilt gold, celestial motifs.

Cyberpunk

High-tech neon — glow, chrome, magenta/cyan, synthwave energy.
